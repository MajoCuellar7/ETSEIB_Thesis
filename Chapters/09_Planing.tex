\chapter{Planning}\label{sec:Planning}

The work presented in this thesis was carried out between February and July 2026, 
divided into four phases: literature review and model development, 
experimental characterisation of the cell, implementation and validation of the 
Extended Kalman Filter algorithms, and thesis writing. Figure~\ref{fig:GanttChart} 
presents the Gantt chart summarising the timeline of the work performed.

The characterisation phase was constrained by the availability of laboratory 
equipment (cycler, power analyser, and setup), which delayed the start of the discharge, 
charge, pulse, and steps tests relative to the initial plan. To keep the project on 
schedule the parameter identification code, the \ac{SOH} estimation algorithm, and 
the corresponding sections of the Methodology chapter were developed in parallel 
while waiting for equipment availability and the setup.
